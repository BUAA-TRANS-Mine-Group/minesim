####  🚀[En]

- Some scenarios from our dynamic scenarios library (in open-pit mines) have been utilized in the "2nd Onsite Autonomous Driving Algorithm Challenge" (Track 2: Unstructured Road Scenarios). 
  - Tthe scenario data and testing tools from MineSim have already been integrated into the Open Naturalistic Simulation and Testing Environment (OnSite), a public platform for online autonomous driving testing developed by Tongji University 
- We have also provided an excellent code environment for closed-loop testing. 
- You can visit our website for more information: [https://www.onsite.com.cn](https://www.onsite.com.cn)
